Universal Installation Guide (Any Launcher)

1. Download the revil_pack.zip (it should have already started). Do not unzip it!.
2. Open Minecraft (TLauncher, Legacy, Prism, or Official).
3. Go to Options → Resource Packs.
4. Click the "Open Pack Folder" button. This works in ALL launchers and opens the correct folder automatically.
5. Drag and drop the revil_pack.zip into the folder that just opened.
6. Go back to the game. Move the pack to the "Selected" column and click Done.
Pro Tip: Если кнопка в игре не срабатывает, используйте путь:
Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\resourcepacks
macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/resourcepacks.

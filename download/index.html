Universal Installation Guide

1. Download the revil_pack.zip (it should have already started). Do not unzip it!
2. Open your Minecraft launcher (TLauncher, Legacy, Prism, or Official) and start the game.
3. In the main menu, go to Options → Resource Packs.
4. Click the "Open Pack Folder" button. This automatically opens the correct folder on your PC.
5. Drag and drop the downloaded revil_pack.zip file into that opened folder.
6. Go back to Minecraft, move the pack to the "Selected" column and click Done.
Pro Tip: Если кнопка в настройках игры не открывает папку, используйте ручной путь:
• Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\resourcepacks
• macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/resourcepacks
